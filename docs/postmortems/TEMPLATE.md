## Summary

State what happened and the impact in plain language. Do not include secrets or unnecessary
personal data.

## Timeline

Use UTC timestamps. Separate observations, decisions, and actions.

## Detection and response

What detected the incident? Which alert/runbook helped or failed? How long did acknowledgement,
diagnosis, mitigation, and recovery take? Do not invent an SLO measurement.

## Contributing conditions

Describe technical and organizational conditions without assigning blame. Include why
existing controls did not prevent or limit the impact.

## Recovery and reconciliation

Record validation of customer paths, orders/payments, inventory, events/DLTs, and data
integrity as applicable.
